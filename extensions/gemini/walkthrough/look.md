## Make it yours

- **Themes:** GeminiCode Dark, Midnight (true black), Dusk (warm greys) and Light.
- **Accent:** blue, violet, rose, teal, amber or the Gemini gradient, for selection, focus, links and the Send button.
- **Code font:** JetBrains Mono and Geist Mono come with GeminiCode; SF Mono and Menlo are the Mac's own.
- **File icons:** GeminiCode's own, tinted by language.

Every choice applies at once. Open the page again any time with **Gemini: Make It Yours**.
