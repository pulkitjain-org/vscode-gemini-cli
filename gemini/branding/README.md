# GeminiCode icons

The mark is the Aperture: a rounded-square frame, a ring and a four-point star, in one ink colour on a paper tile (ink `#16150F` on paper `#F4F2EC`), or ivory `#F1EEE6` on charcoal `#1C1B18` for dark surfaces. No gradient, no glow and no accent colour: the Gemini colours stay inside the app for Gemini's own moments. The design and its rules are on the site redesign canvas, Identity artboard.

- `GeminiCode.icon`: the macOS app icon as an Icon Composer document (open it with Xcode's Icon Composer). `icon.json` is the paper fill with a charcoal specialization for the dark appearance, and one group of three vector layers, `Assets/frame.svg`, `ring.svg` and `star.svg`, on the 1024 canvas. Each layer's fill is specialized per appearance: ink by default, ivory for `dark` and `tinted` (every specialization list starts with its base entry, which has no `appearance`; without it the list is ignored). The layers are flat (`glass` off): Liquid Glass stays on the tile only, so the strokes keep their contrast in Dark, and the Clear and Tinted Dock styles show the mark instead of a faint glass relief. `preview/` holds the six macOS renditions (Default, Dark, Tinted and Clear in light and dark) as the script last rendered them.
- `icon-macos.svg`: the classic icon, the same mark on Apple's 824/1024 tile with continuous corners, a light rim and a soft shadow, for `resources/darwin/code.icns` (the `CFBundleIconFile`, the `.dmg` window and anything that still reads `.icns`).
- `icon.svg`: the flat 256 icon for the web and `code-icon.svg` in the workbench. Windows and Linux are not built, so their rasters are not regenerated.

Run `gemini/scripts/make-icons.sh` on macOS 26 with Xcode 26 after changing any of these. It compiles `GeminiCode.icon` with `actool` into `resources/darwin/Assets.car`, renders `icon-macos.svg` per size (never scaled down from one large image) into `resources/darwin/code.icns`, and copies `icon.svg` into the workbench. The release workflow copies `Assets.car` into the app and sets `CFBundleIconName`; see [`gemini-release.yml`](../../.github/workflows/gemini-release.yml).
