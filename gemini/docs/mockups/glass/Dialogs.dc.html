Pop-ups and dialogs

Each dialog is a glass panel: the opaque pop-up colour with its sheen, a rim highlight and one deep shadow, over the window dimmed by a scrim. The scrim does not blur: a dialog can open over a chat that is still streaming, and blurring the whole window would re-rasterise on every chunk. Fields are glass wells; the focused one takes the accent ring. The merge confirmation is the workbench's own dialog, already in-app; it takes the same glass.
