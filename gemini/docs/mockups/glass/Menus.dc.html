Menus and pickers

Every menu is the same glass: an opaque pop-up colour with a light sheen at its top, a rim highlight and one shadow. Rows have an icon, a name, a detail under it and an end note; the chosen row has the lens fill and a tick in the accent. Nothing blurs: a blurred menu over a streaming chat would re-run the blur on every chunk, and at a 92% tint it showed almost nothing. Text sits on a known colour, so contrast is measured as it is today.
