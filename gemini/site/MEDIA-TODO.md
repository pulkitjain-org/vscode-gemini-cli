# Media still needed for the site

Every screenshot and clip in `gemini/site/media/` comes from the real app, except `showcase-console-*` (kept from an earlier build by choice). They were taken in GeminiCode Dark and GeminiCode Light, with the window filling the built-in display at 2x, and exported at 2000 × 1250. Each product shot has a `-dark.webp` and a `-light.webp`; `build.mts` shows the one that matches the page's theme.

The feature pages' shots (`home`, `index-page`, `files`, `docs/plus-menu`, `mr-review-diff`, `jira-comment`) and the `showcase-work` and `showcase-mr-review` clips were taken on 2026-10-23 against the demo in `demo/`, signed in as the demo's Sam Rivera. The others were taken on 2026-10-19.

Capture from the real app, against the demo in `demo/`: never redraw or reuse a shot from this folder for a different purpose. `demo/README.md` says how to set up the demo and take each shot.
